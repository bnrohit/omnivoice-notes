Privacy & compatibility

  • Recordings are saved in this browser's IndexedDB by default.
  • Audio is sent to the configured server only when you tap Summarize.
  • No API key is stored in browser code.
  • The app never auto-starts recording or tries to hide the recording state.
  • Desktop system/tab audio depends on browser support and the source you select. Mobile browsers generally cannot capture phone calls or audio from other apps.
